# Phase 06: Services & Capabilities Showcase

## Context
Build the `/services` page detailing the full range of AI creative services offered by LENA.

## Objectives
1. Implement detailed breakdown for all 7 service offerings:
   - AI Video Production
   - AI Advertising & Commercials
   - AI Product Videos & 3D Fluid Dynamics
   - AI UGC (User-Generated Content)
   - Cinematic AI Films & Trailers
   - Social Media Velocity Packs
   - Custom AI Video Engagements
2. List core deliverables, ideal use cases, tools used, and turnaround times for each service.
3. Provide direct inquiry links per service.

## Allowed Files
- `src/app/services/page.tsx`
- `src/data/services.json`

## Acceptance Criteria
- All 7 services clearly display deliverables and use-case tags.
- Direct inquiry buttons pre-populate the contact experience.
