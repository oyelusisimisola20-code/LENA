## Context
Execute a rigorous cross-device, cross-browser, and accessibility audit to ensure the portfolio operates flawlessly.

## Objectives
1. Verify layout across 375px (mobile), 768px (tablet), 1280px (laptop), and 1920px+ (desktop).
